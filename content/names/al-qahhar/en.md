Al-Qahhar is the Subduer — the One whose power overcomes everything, so completely that nothing can resist or escape it.

## What the name means

Qahr in Arabic is to overpower and bring under control. The intensive form means this is total: every creature is subdued by death, by need, by the limits it did not choose.

The Quran pairs it almost always with oneness — al-Wahid al-Qahhar. Absolute power and absolute unity belong together: what is truly irresistible cannot be shared.

## Living by this name

It answers the tyrant. Whatever power a person holds is itself held, and the Quran shows Yusuf ﷺ using this name in prison to argue that scattered lords are no lords at all.

Its fruit is to subdue the self rather than others — the harder conquest the Prophet ﷺ pointed to. See also [[al-jabbar]] and [[al-wahid]].
