Ad-Darr is the Distresser — the One by whose decree harm may reach whom He wills, never outside His wisdom.

## What the name means

Darr is harm. Scholars are careful here: the name is never used alone, because Allah's acts are wholly good even when their effect on a servant is painful, and evil is not attributed to Him as a description.

It is not in the Quran as a noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, always paired with [[an-nafi]].

## Living by this name

The Quran makes the pairing practical: if Allah touches you with harm, none can remove it but He. The one who permits it is the only address for relief.

Its response is to seek refuge in Him from Him. See also [[an-nafi]] and [[al-hakim]].
