Al-Mudhill is the Humbler — the One who brings low whom He wills, always with justice and never with wrong.

## What the name means

Dhull is lowliness. As a divine act it is the withdrawal of a support that was never owed, and the Quran states it in the same breath as its opposite: You honour whom You will and You abase whom You will — in Your hand is all good.

That last clause is deliberate: even the abasing is placed inside the good, because it is corrective rather than cruel.

## Living by this name

It warns against reading someone's fall as proof of Allah's hatred, or one's own standing as proof of His love. Both are tests, and the Quran says so explicitly of wealth and of hardship.

Its response is fear of arrogance rather than fear of people. See also [[al-mu-izz]] and [[al-khafid]].
