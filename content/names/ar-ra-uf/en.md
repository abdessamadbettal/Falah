Ar-Ra'uf is the Most Kind — compassion in its most tender form, that spares before harm arrives.

## What the name means

Ra'fah is the gentlest degree of mercy. Scholars describe it as mercy that prevents difficulty, where rahmah also relieves it after it comes.

The Quran pairs the two — Ra'uf, Rahim — and uses the same pairing for the Prophet ﷺ toward the believers, the only creature so described.

## Living by this name

It appears where a burden is lifted rather than imposed: the change of qiblah closes with the assurance that Allah is Ra'uf, Rahim toward people.

Its response is gentleness that anticipates, not only consoles. See also [[ar-rahim]] and [[al-latif]].
