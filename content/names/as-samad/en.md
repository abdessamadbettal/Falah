As-Samad is the Eternal Refuge — the One all creation turns to in need, who Himself needs no one.

## What the name means

The early scholars gave two readings, and both are sound: the One to whom all turn with their needs, and the One who is solid — without hollow, without hunger, without dependency.

Together they explain why He alone can be relied on: what has needs of its own cannot carry yours.

## Living by this name

It is the second verse of Surah al-Ikhlas, placed immediately after oneness. Being one is not enough for a deity; being sufficient is what makes the one worth turning to.

Its response is to bring the whole need, not the part you think is reasonable. See also [[al-ahad]] and [[al-ghaniyy]].
