Ar-Rafi' is the Exalter — the One who raises in rank whom He wills, by faith and knowledge rather than by wealth.

## What the name means

Raf' is to lift. In the Quran the criterion of elevation is stated plainly: Allah raises those who believe among you, and those given knowledge, by degrees.

The name itself is not in the Quran as a standalone noun — it enters the list of ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the act appears throughout.

## Living by this name

It relocates ambition. The rank that matters is not granted by an institution, and the Prophet ﷺ said that no one humbles himself for Allah except that Allah raises him.

Its response is to pursue what is actually elevating: knowledge acted upon, and humility. See also [[al-khafid]] and [[al-ali]].
