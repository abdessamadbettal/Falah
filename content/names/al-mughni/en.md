Al-Mughni is the Enricher — the One who frees His servants from need, of wealth and of heart.

## What the name means

Ighna is to make someone free of need. The name is the giving form of [[al-ghaniyy]]: He is rich in Himself, and He enriches others.

It is not in the Quran as a divine noun — it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi — while the act is stated: He is the One who enriched and made content.

## Living by this name

The two verbs in that verse are separate on purpose. To be given much and not be content is not enrichment; the second is the part that has to be asked for.

Its response is to ask for sufficiency rather than for a number. See also [[al-ghaniyy]] and [[ar-razzaq]].
