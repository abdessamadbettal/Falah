As-Sami' is the All-Hearing — the One who hears every sound at once, and hears the prayer that is never spoken aloud.

## What the name means

Sam' is hearing, but the Quranic sense includes responding: to say Allah hears the one who praises Him is to say He answers, not merely that sound reaches Him.

Aisha reported her wonder that Allah heard the woman who disputed with the Prophet ﷺ from a corner of the room — and Surah al-Mujadilah opens by confirming it.

## Living by this name

It removes the need to perform du'a. Nothing is gained by volume, and nothing is lost by silence, because hearing is not limited by distance or noise.

It also disciplines speech: the tongue is never off the record. See also [[al-basir]] and [[al-mujib]].
