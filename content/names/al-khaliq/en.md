Al-Khaliq is the Creator — the One who brings things into being from nothing, by measure and without a prior model.

## What the name means

Khalq in Arabic carries both origination and proportioning. To create is not only to make something exist but to give it exact measure. The Quran says He created everything and determined it with precise determination.

It is the first of three names that appear together — Creator, Originator, Fashioner — describing one act in three stages: to decree, to bring forth, to shape.

## Living by this name

Nothing a person makes is creation in this sense; every craft rearranges what already exists. The name marks the line between the Maker and everything made.

Its practical fruit is contentment with how you were made, and reverence for what you did not make. See also [[al-bari]] and [[al-musawwir]].
