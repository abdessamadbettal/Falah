Al-Mujib is the Responsive — the One who answers the one who calls on Him, near and without intermediary.

## What the name means

Ijabah is response. The name says answering is not occasional but characteristic: Salih ﷺ describes his Lord as qarib mujib — near and responsive — in a single phrase that removes both distance and delay.

The Quran states the mechanism elsewhere with no conditions attached: call upon Me, I will respond to you.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ described three forms an answer takes: it is given, or it is stored for the hereafter, or an equivalent harm is turned away. On that account no du'a is refused, though not every du'a is granted as asked.

Its response is to keep asking, including for what seems small. See also [[as-sami]] and [[al-wasi]].
