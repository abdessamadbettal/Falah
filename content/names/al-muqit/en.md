Al-Muqit is the Sustainer — the One who apportions nourishment to every creature and has power over all of it.

## What the name means

Qut in Arabic is the measure of food that keeps a body alive — not abundance, but exactly enough. The name therefore joins provision with precision: what is given is weighed.

The single verse carrying it uses the sense of oversight too: Allah is ever, over all things, Muqit — able, watchful, and in charge of what He apportions.

## Living by this name

It answers the fear of running out. What sustains you is measured by the One who measures, and the Prophet ﷺ warned that it is enough sin for a person to neglect those he is obliged to feed.

Its response is to hold provision as a trust rather than a possession. See also [[ar-razzaq]] and [[al-hasib]].
