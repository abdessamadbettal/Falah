Al-Quddus is the Utterly Pure — free of every defect, and far above whatever the mind reaches for when it tries to picture Him.

## What the name means

The root q-d-s carries purity and separation together — the sense of something set entirely apart because nothing has touched it. Al-Quddus is therefore not merely without fault; He is beyond the categories in which fault is even measured.

Classical scholars read the name as the answer to every mental image: whatever you imagine, Allah is other than that. It guards the doctrine of tanzih, that Allah is exalted above resemblance to His creation.

## Living by this name

The name asks a worshipper to purify what can be purified — intention first, then the tongue and the limbs. The connection is not decorative: the Quran ties success to tazkiyah, the purification of the self.

It also disciplines how we speak about Allah. To say of Him only what He and His Messenger ﷺ said of Him is the practical form of holding this name in mind.
