Al-Batin is the Hidden — nearer than anything, yet beyond the reach of perception.

## What the name means

Butun is interiority and closeness. The name is not a claim of absence but of nearness so complete that nothing stands between: the Prophet ﷺ said, You are the Hidden, so there is nothing below You.

Held with az-Zahir it prevents two errors: that Allah is remote, and that He can be grasped.

## Living by this name

The Quran states the nearness without intermediary: when My servants ask you about Me, I am near. The answer skips the messenger and speaks in the first person.

Its response is du'a spoken directly, without brokers. See also [[az-zahir]] and [[al-mujib]].
