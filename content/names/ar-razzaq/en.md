Ar-Razzaq is the Provider — the One who sustains every living thing, the obedient and the disobedient alike.

## What the name means

Rizq is wider than food or money: it is anything that benefits, including knowledge, health, companionship and time. The intensive form says provision is continuous and universal, not occasional.

The single verse that carries the name states the reason: Allah is the Provider, the Firm in strength — provision follows from power, not from need on His part.

## Living by this name

It removes the panic from earning. The Prophet ﷺ said that if people relied on Allah as He deserves, they would be provided as the birds are — leaving hungry, returning full. Reliance and effort are joined, not opposed.

It also forbids seeking provision by forbidden means: what is guaranteed does not need to be stolen. See also [[al-wahhab]] and [[al-muqit]].
