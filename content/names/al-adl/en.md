Al-Adl is the Utterly Just — the One who is justice itself, who wrongs no one by even the weight of an atom.

## What the name means

Arabic does not say Allah is adil, one who is just, but al-Adl, justice itself — the standard rather than something measured against a standard.

The name is not in the Quran as a divine noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the attribute is everywhere: Allah does not wrong even the weight of an atom, and your Lord wrongs no one.

## Living by this name

Because the standard is Allah's, justice is not owed only to those we like. The Quran commands that hatred of a people must not push you into injustice — be just, it is nearer to piety.

Its response is fairness that survives your own interest. See also [[al-hakam]] and [[al-muqsit]].
