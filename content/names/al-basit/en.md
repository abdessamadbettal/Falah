Al-Basit is the Extender — the One who spreads out provision, mercy and relief for whom He wills.

## What the name means

Bast is to spread open, the way a hand opens or a cloth is unrolled. It covers widening of sustenance, of the chest in contentment, and of hope after despair.

The Quran gives it as a verb beside its pair: Allah withholds and extends, and to Him you will be returned. The two are one policy seen from two sides.

## Living by this name

Expansion is a test as much as restriction is. The Quran warns that if Allah extended provision to His servants without measure they would transgress — so what is withheld may be protection.

Its response is gratitude that spends rather than hoards. See also [[al-qabid]] and [[al-wasi]].
