Ash-Shakur is the Most Appreciative — the One who rewards a small deed many times over and never overlooks it.

## What the name means

Shukr from a servant is acknowledgement of a gift. From Allah it means accepting the little and multiplying it — gratitude expressed as reward rather than as need.

The Quran states the exchange: whoever brings a good deed will have ten like it, and elsewhere describes Allah as Ghafur Shakur — forgiving the failures and thanking the efforts.

## Living by this name

It changes what counts as worth doing. A small consistent act is not too small to register, and the Prophet ﷺ said the most beloved deeds are the most constant, even if few.

Its response is gratitude that is spoken and acted, not merely felt. See also [[al-hamid]] and [[al-karim]].
