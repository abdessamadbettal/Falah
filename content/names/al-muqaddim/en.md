Al-Muqaddim is the Expediter — the One who advances whom and what He wills, in time and in rank.

## What the name means

Taqdim is to put forward. Allah advances some people over others in knowledge, in provision, in guidance — and advances events to their appointed moment.

The name is not in the Quran as a noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, and the Prophet ﷺ used it in du'a: You are the One who brings forward and the One who puts back.

## Living by this name

It settles envy at the root. Precedence is assigned, not seized, and the Quran forbids wishing for what Allah gave others in preference.

Its response is to compete in what is open to everyone — righteousness. See also [[al-mu-akhkhir]] and [[ar-rafi]].
