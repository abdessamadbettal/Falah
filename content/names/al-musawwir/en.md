Al-Musawwir is the Fashioner — the One who gives every created thing its own distinct form.

## What the name means

Surah in Arabic is form, shape, appearance. This is the third stage after decree and origination: the particular face, the particular voice, the pattern on a leaf that belongs to no other leaf.

The Quran says He shapes you in the wombs however He wills — variety by intention rather than by accident.

## Living by this name

It is the ground for treating difference as design. Colour, feature and form are the work of the Fashioner, which is why the Quran lists the variety of human tongues and colours among His signs rather than among His accidents.

It also explains the gravity with which the Sunnah treats imitating creation in image-making. See also [[al-khaliq]] and [[al-bari]].
