Al-Hadi is the Guide — the One who shows the way and grants the heart that follows it.

## What the name means

Hidayah in the Quran has two levels: showing the path, which is offered to everyone, and granting arrival on it, which belongs to Allah alone.

The distinction is stated to the Prophet ﷺ himself: you do not guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills — the limit of even a messenger.

## Living by this name

It is why the most repeated du'a in Islam is a request for guidance, recited in every unit of every prayer: guide us to the straight path.

Its response is to ask for guidance for those you cannot persuade. See also [[an-nur]] and [[ar-rashid]].
