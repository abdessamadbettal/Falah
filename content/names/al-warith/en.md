Al-Warith is the Inheritor — to whom everything returns when its holders have gone.

## What the name means

An heir receives what an owner leaves. Allah is al-Warith in the exact sense: every possession passes, and He remains its owner when no claimant is left.

The Quran gives it in the plural of majesty — it is We who give life and cause death, and We are the Inheritors — rather than as the singular definite name, which is why it reaches the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi.

## Living by this name

Zakariyya ﷺ used it while childless: my Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors. He asked for a son while conceding the outcome.

Its response is to hold property as a steward. See also [[al-baqi]] and [[malik-ul-mulk]].
