Al-Qabid is the Withholder — the One who narrows provision, holds back, and takes the soul, always by wisdom rather than by whim.

## What the name means

Qabd is to grasp and to close the hand. It covers restricting sustenance, withholding a request, and taking a life at its appointed time. The Quran states it as a verb, paired with its opposite: Allah withholds and He extends.

The pairing is the point. Neither name is read alone, because a hand that only closed would not be mercy and a hand that only opened would not be wisdom.

## Living by this name

It teaches how to read a loss. A door closed by the Withholder is closed on purpose, and the Quran attaches the promise of ease to hardship rather than to its absence.

Its response is patience without resentment, and asking again. See also [[al-basit]] and [[al-hakim]].
