Al-Qayyum is the Self-Subsisting Sustainer — existing by Himself, and holding everything else in existence.

## What the name means

Qiyam in Arabic is standing and maintaining. The name says two things at once: Allah stands in need of nothing, and everything else stands only because He maintains it.

The verse of the Throne states the second half directly: neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him, and preserving the heavens and the earth does not tire Him.

## Living by this name

It removes the picture of a creator who set things going and withdrew. Existence is continuously held, not wound up once.

Its response is dependence expressed in du'a. The Prophet ﷺ began the night prayer by calling Allah qayyimu-s-samawati wal-ard. See also [[al-hayy]] and [[al-wakil]].
