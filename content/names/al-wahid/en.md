Al-Wahid is the One — single in His essence, His attributes and His right to be worshipped, with no partner.

## What the name means

Wahdaniyyah is oneness that excludes partnership. The Quran uses this name in the argument against many lords: are separate lords better, or Allah, al-Wahid al-Qahhar?

It is the doctrinal centre of Islam. Every other name is understood through it, because an attribute shared would not be divine.

## Living by this name

Tawhid is not only a belief but a simplification of life: one to please, one to fear, one to ask.

Its response is to stop dividing the heart between authorities that cannot agree. See also [[al-ahad]] and [[al-qahhar]].
