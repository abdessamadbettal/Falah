Al-Wahhab is the Bestower — the One who gives freely, without being asked and without expecting anything in return.

## What the name means

A hibah in Arabic is a gift given for nothing: no price, no exchange, no obligation created. The intensive form makes it constant — giving as a standing habit rather than an occasional act.

It distinguishes this name from provision. A razzaq feeds what he sustains; a wahhab gives what was never owed at all.

## Living by this name

The Quran records it in du'a more than in description: our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate, and grant us mercy from Yourself — You are al-Wahhab. Sulayman ﷺ used it when asking for a kingdom, and the Quran preserves both requests.

It teaches asking largely, and giving without keeping accounts. See also [[ar-razzaq]] and [[al-karim]].
