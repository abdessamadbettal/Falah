Al-Ba'ith is the Resurrector — the One who raises the dead to life on the Day of Judgement, and who sends prophets to the living.

## What the name means

Ba'th in Arabic is to send forth and to raise up. The Quran uses the verb for both the sending of messengers and the raising of the dead, which is why the name carries a double weight.

The name is not in the Quran as a standalone noun — it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi — while the act is stated repeatedly: Allah will raise those in the graves.

## Living by this name

It supplies the answer to the objection the Quran quotes most often: who will give life to bones when they are disintegrated? The reply is that the One who produced them the first time will.

Its response is to live as someone who will be raised and asked. See also [[al-mu-id]] and [[al-muhyi]].
