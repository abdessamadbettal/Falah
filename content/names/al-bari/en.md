Al-Bari' is the One who brings creation into being free of fault, each part fitted exactly to its purpose.

## What the name means

The root b-r-' means to be free of something — the same root as bara'ah, acquittal. Al-Bari' produces creatures free of disproportion: nothing superfluous, nothing missing, nothing at odds with the rest.

The Quran invites the test directly: look again at the sky, and look again — your sight returns to you exhausted, having found no flaw.

## Living by this name

It answers the complaint against one's own body or circumstances. What looks like a defect is measured against a purpose you may not have been shown.

It also asks for care in whatever you make: excellence in work is an echo of the attribute. See also [[al-khaliq]] and [[al-musawwir]].
