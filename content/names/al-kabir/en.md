Al-Kabir is the Most Great — greater than everything, so that beside Him nothing retains its scale.

## What the name means

Kibriya is grandeur of rank. The Quran uses this name where it contrasts Allah with what people worship or fear instead: that is because Allah is the Truth, and what they call upon besides Him is falsehood, and Allah is al-Ali, al-Kabir.

The pairing with al-Ali is consistent, and the argument in each place is the same: size everything else correctly by seeing Him first.

## Living by this name

It is the meaning packed into the takbir. Saying Allahu akbar at the start of every prayer is a declaration that whatever you left outside the prayer is smaller than what you have entered.

Its response is to stop being intimidated by what is, in fact, small. See also [[al-ali]] and [[al-mutakabbir]].
