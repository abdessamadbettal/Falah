Ash-Shahid is the Witness — present to everything, and whose testimony settles what no one else saw.

## What the name means

Shahadah joins presence and testimony: to witness is to have been there and to be able to testify. Allah is Shahid in both — nothing occurs outside His presence, and His record needs no corroboration.

The Quran uses it as the closing argument in disputes: sufficient is Allah as a witness between us and you.

## Living by this name

It is the recourse of anyone whose account is disbelieved. The witness who matters was present, and the Quran repeatedly ends contested matters with that fact.

It also weighs against false testimony and against private wrong. See also [[ar-raqib]] and [[al-hasib]].
