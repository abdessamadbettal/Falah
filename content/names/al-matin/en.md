Al-Matin is the Firm — strength that is solid and unyielding, which no effort wears down.

## What the name means

Matn in Arabic is the firm ground, the solid back of a thing. Where al-Qawiyy names the strength itself, al-Matin names its steadiness — power that does not slacken.

The two meet in the verse on provision: Allah is the Provider, Owner of Power, the Firm — sustaining all creation without strain.

## Living by this name

It reassures anyone whose need is long rather than sudden. Sustained provision does not exhaust the One providing it.

Its response is steadiness in worship — the Prophet ﷺ preferred the constant deed to the intense one. See also [[al-qawiyy]] and [[ar-razzaq]].
