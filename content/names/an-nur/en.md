An-Nur is the Light — the Light of the heavens and the earth, by whom what is hidden becomes clear.

## What the name means

Nur is light. Scholars read the name on more than one level: Allah is light in Himself, He is the source of all light, and He is the one who gives guidance, which the Quran repeatedly calls light.

The verse that names Him goes straight into an image — a niche, a lamp, a glass like a brilliant star — because light is easier shown than defined.

## Living by this name

Guidance in the Quran is consistently a movement out of darkness into light, always in the singular: one light, many darknesses.

Its response is to ask for light, as the Prophet ﷺ did — in the heart, the hearing, the sight, and every direction around him. See also [[al-hadi]] and [[az-zahir]].
