Al-Karim is the Most Generous — the One who gives before being asked, and who does not reproach the one who asks.

## What the name means

Karam in Arabic joins two things: abundance in giving and nobility in the manner of it. A karim gives much, gives without being begged, and does not remind you afterwards that he gave.

The Quran uses the name at the moment of confronting human ingratitude: O man, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Karim? The choice of name is the argument.

## Living by this name

It shapes how a believer asks: generously, of the Generous, without shrinking the request to what seems reasonable.

It also shapes how a believer gives — without publicising it, which the Quran says cancels charity. See also [[al-wahhab]] and [[al-barr]].
