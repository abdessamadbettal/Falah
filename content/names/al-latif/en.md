Al-Latif is the Subtle and Kind — the One whose knowledge reaches the finest detail and whose kindness arrives unnoticed.

## What the name means

Lutf carries two senses that Arabic keeps together: fineness, so subtle it escapes perception, and gentleness. Allah is Latif in both — He knows what is too small to be seen, and He is gentle with His servants in ways they do not detect.

The Quran joins the two directly: vision does not reach Him and He reaches vision, and He is al-Latif, al-Khabir.

## Living by this name

It reframes an unwanted outcome as possible kindness in a form not yet recognised. Yusuf ﷺ used the name at the end of his story: my Lord is Latif in what He wills — after the well, the slavery and the prison.

Its response is gentleness in dealing with people. See also [[al-khabir]] and [[ar-ra-uf]].
