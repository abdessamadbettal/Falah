Al-Basir is the All-Seeing — the One who sees everything, including what is hidden inside a person.

## What the name means

Basar is sight, but the Quran uses this name for insight as well as vision: Allah sees the act and the motive behind it, the deed done publicly and the one buried in the dark.

It is almost always paired with hearing — as-Sami' al-Basir — because together they close every gap: nothing is said that is unheard, nothing done that is unseen.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ defined ihsan as worshipping Allah as though you see Him, and if you do not see Him, knowing that He sees you. This name is the second half of that definition.

Its fruit is consistency: the private self and the public self become the same person. See also [[as-sami]] and [[ar-raqib]].
