Al-Muqsit is the Equitable — who gives every party its due and restores what was taken.

## What the name means

Qist is equity, giving each their share. The Arabic form here means to establish equity, distinct from qasata which means to deviate from it — the same root pointing opposite ways.

The name is not in the Quran as a divine noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the Quran states that Allah loves those who act with qist.

## Living by this name

It makes justice an act of worship rather than a policy. The Quran commands standing firmly for justice even against yourself, your parents and your relatives.

Its response is fairness in the small transactions nobody audits. See also [[al-adl]] and [[al-hakam]].
