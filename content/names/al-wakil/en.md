Al-Wakil is the Trustee — the One to whom affairs are handed over, and who manages them better than their owner could.

## What the name means

A wakil in Arabic is an agent given full authority to act on another's behalf. To take Allah as wakil is to transfer the file, not to add Him as an adviser.

The Quran preserves the words the believers said under threat: sufficient for us is Allah, and He is the best Trustee — and records that they returned with favour from Allah.

## Living by this name

Tawakkul is not the abandonment of means. The Prophet ﷺ told the man with the camel to tie it and then trust, joining the two in one sentence.

Its response is to do what is yours and hand over what is not. See also [[al-hasib]] and [[al-hafiz]].
