Al-Muhsi is the All-Enumerating — the One who knows the exact number of everything, with nothing rounded and nothing lost.

## What the name means

Ihsa is counting precisely, the same word used in the hadith about the ninety-nine names. Allah's enumeration is not a tally He keeps but knowledge that is already complete.

The name is not in the Quran as a noun — it reaches the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi — while the act is stated plainly: He has enumerated them and counted them a full counting.

## Living by this name

It answers the fear that a small good deed was too small to matter, and the hope that a small wrong was too small to notice. Neither is true.

Its response is care with what seems minor. See also [[al-hasib]] and [[al-alim]].
