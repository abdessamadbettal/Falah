Al-Wasi is the All-Encompassing — whose knowledge, mercy and bounty are wide enough that nothing falls outside them.

## What the name means

Sa'ah in Arabic is breadth and capacity. The name is never abstract in the Quran: it is attached to what is wide — wide in mercy, wide in provision, wide in knowledge — so the breadth always has an object.

It appears where the Quran removes a limit people had assumed: wherever you turn, there is the Face of Allah; indeed Allah is Wasi, Alim.

## Living by this name

It corrects a small view of forgiveness or of provision. Allah promises to enrich from His breadth, and the Quran ties spending to that promise rather than to your balance.

Its response is to ask without rationing the request. See also [[al-ghaniyy]] and [[al-karim]].
