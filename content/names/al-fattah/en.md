Al-Fattah is the Opener — the One who opens what is shut, and who judges between people with the truth.

## What the name means

Fath in Arabic covers both opening and deciding; an Arab judge was said to open the case between two parties. So the name works on two levels: doors opened — provision, understanding, relief — and disputes settled.

The single verse carrying it uses the judging sense: our Lord will bring us together, then open between us with the truth, and He is al-Fattah, al-Alim.

## Living by this name

It is the name for what looks closed: a shut door in work, in marriage, in understanding a text. The Opener is not obliged to use the door you were watching.

It also reframes injustice you cannot settle — an open case is not a lost one. See also [[al-alim]] and [[al-hakam]].
