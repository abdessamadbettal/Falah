Al-Mutakabbir is the One rightfully supreme — greatness that belongs to Him alone, and to which no creature has any claim.

## What the name means

The form implies claiming greatness. In a human being that claim is false and the Quran condemns it; in Allah it is simply accurate. He is above every attribute of creation, above need, above resemblance.

It closes the sequence in Surah al-Hashr — the King, the Holy, the Peace, the Giver of Security, the Guardian, the Almighty, the Compeller, the Supreme — a single verse naming eight.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ reported that Allah says greatness is His cloak, and whoever contests Him for it is punished. Knowing this name is therefore the cure for pride: the attribute is taken, and no one else may wear it.

Its fruit is humility before Allah and gentleness with people. See also [[al-jabbar]] and [[al-ali]].
