Al-Ali is the Most High — exalted above His creation in essence, in power and in rank.

## What the name means

Uluw in Arabic is height, but the Quran uses it of status and dominance as much as of place. Allah is high above resemblance, above need, and above anything a mind can place beside Him.

It closes the verse of the Throne together with al-Azim, and appears again with al-Kabir — height and greatness stated as one idea.

## Living by this name

It orders the heart's hierarchy: what is above everything cannot be second to anything, and the Quran treats rivalry for that place as the one unforgivable wrong.

Its response is to lower yourself in prostration, the posture the Prophet ﷺ called the closest a servant comes to his Lord. See also [[al-azim]] and [[al-muta-ali]].
