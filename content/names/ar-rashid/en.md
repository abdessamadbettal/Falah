Ar-Rashid is the Guide to the Right Path — whose management of every affair is sound, needing no correction.

## What the name means

Rushd in Arabic is soundness of judgement, the opposite of ghayy, misdirection. Applied to Allah it means His directing of creation is right in itself — not guidance offered, but wisdom enacted.

The name is not in the Quran as a divine noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, and the Quran uses rushd of the guidance He grants.

## Living by this name

It is why the Quran ties rushd to being shown the way: whoever Allah guides is rightly guided, and whoever He leaves astray will find no guiding protector.

Its response is istikharah — asking the One whose direction is sound to choose. See also [[al-hadi]] and [[al-hakim]].
