Al-Mani' is the Preventer — the One who withholds and defends, protecting by refusal as much as by giving.

## What the name means

Man' is to prevent, and in Arabic also to defend a thing from being reached. Both senses apply: Allah withholds what would harm, and shields what He chooses to keep.

The name is not in the Quran as a noun — it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi — while the meaning appears in the Prophet's ﷺ words after every prayer: there is no preventer of what You have given, and no giver of what You have withheld.

## Living by this name

That supplication removes the middlemen. If nothing given can be blocked and nothing withheld can be forced, then approval and opposition both lose their weight.

Its response is to stop lobbying creation for what only One decides. See also [[al-qabid]] and [[al-basit]].
