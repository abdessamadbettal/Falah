Al-Azim is the Magnificent — great in a way that has no upper limit and admits no comparison.

## What the name means

Azamah is greatness of rank and being, not of size. The Quran attaches the name to the verse of the Throne, where after describing His knowledge and dominion it closes: and He is al-Ali, al-Azim.

Scholars note that the name is stated after the description rather than before it: the greatness is the conclusion the verse has just proved.

## Living by this name

It sets the tone of prayer. The Prophet ﷺ taught the bowing formula subhana rabbiyal-azim — glory to my Lord, the Magnificent — so the body's lowest posture is paired with the highest attribute.

Its response is to right-size your troubles against the One you are asking. See also [[al-ali]] and [[al-kabir]].
