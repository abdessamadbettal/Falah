Al-Haqq is the Absolute Truth — the One whose existence is real in a way nothing else's is, and whose word does not fail.

## What the name means

Haqq in Arabic is what is established and does not pass away, the opposite of batil — the void, the thing that dissolves when examined. Allah is al-Haqq because His being is underived: everything else exists on loan.

The Quran states the contrast as the whole argument: that is because Allah is al-Haqq, and what they call upon besides Him is falsehood.

## Living by this name

It gives a test for what deserves a life. Whatever ends is not the thing to spend a life on, and the Quran repeatedly measures the world against this name.

Its response is truthfulness — the Prophet ﷺ tied truthfulness to righteousness and righteousness to Paradise. See also [[al-hakam]] and [[al-baqi]].
