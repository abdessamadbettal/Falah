Az-Zahir is the Manifest — evident above everything, known through signs that leave no excuse.

## What the name means

Zuhur is both appearing and prevailing. Allah is Manifest because the evidence for Him is everywhere, and because He is above all — the same word carries both senses.

The Prophet ﷺ explained it as height: You are the Manifest, so there is nothing above You.

## Living by this name

It answers the claim that faith requires seeing. What is invisible is not thereby hidden: the Quran argues from the ordinary — rain, seed, sleep, the alternation of night and day.

Its response is to read the world as evidence rather than scenery. See also [[al-batin]] and [[an-nur]].
