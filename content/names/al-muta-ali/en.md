Al-Muta'ali is the Most Exalted — high above every deficiency and above whatever is said of Him without knowledge.

## What the name means

The form intensifies [[al-ali]]: not only high, but rising above every attempt to bring Him down to a comparison.

The Quran places it after describing His knowledge of the hidden and the seen: the Great, the Most Exalted — greatness stated, then raised beyond reach.

## Living by this name

It guards how we speak of Allah. The Quran condemns saying about Him what you do not know as a sin listed beside injustice.

Its response is to speak of Allah from evidence, and to stay silent where there is none. See also [[al-ali]] and [[al-quddus]].
