Al-Hasib is the Reckoner — the One who takes account of every deed, and who is Himself sufficient for His servant.

## What the name means

The root h-s-b carries two meanings the Quran uses together: to reckon, and to suffice. Allah is Hasib because He counts exactly, and because hasbi Allah — Allah is enough for me — needs no addition.

The reckoning is described as fast and complete: sufficient is Allah as a reckoner, said of a record that misses nothing.

## Living by this name

It makes self-audit reasonable now rather than later. Umar's reported counsel — call yourselves to account before you are called to account — is this name applied.

Its second meaning is the cure for anxiety about outcomes: what suffices you is not a quantity. See also [[al-wakil]] and [[al-muqit]].
