Al-Muntaqim is the Avenger — the One who justly requites those who persist in wrongdoing after warning.

## What the name means

Intiqam in Arabic is retribution taken by right, not resentment. Applied to Allah it excludes the human element entirely: no anger that clouds, no score being settled for a personal loss.

The name is not in the Quran as a noun — it reaches the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi — while the verb appears with a condition attached: We take retribution from the criminals.

## Living by this name

The condition is what makes it mercy. Retribution follows persistence after clear warning, which is why the Quran's threats always arrive after its invitations.

Its response is to leave your own vengeance to the One who judges. See also [[al-adl]] and [[al-afuww]].
