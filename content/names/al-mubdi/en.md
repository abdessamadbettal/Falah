Al-Mubdi' is the Initiator — the One who begins creation from nothing, with no model to copy.

## What the name means

Ibda is to start something that had no precedent. The name marks the first act, the one no creature can imitate: bringing being out of non-being rather than rearranging what exists.

It is not a standalone noun in the Quran — it enters through at-Tirmidhi — while the verb is constant, always paired with the return: He begins creation, then repeats it.

## Living by this name

That pairing is the Quran's argument for resurrection: whoever could begin can repeat, and repeating is the easier of the two.

Its response is confidence that a beginning is always possible, including a second one. See also [[al-mu-id]] and [[al-badi]].
