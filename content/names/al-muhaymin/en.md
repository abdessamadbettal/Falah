Al-Muhaymin is the Watchful Guardian — the One whose oversight covers everything and whose custody nothing escapes.

## What the name means

Muhaymin combines three ideas Arabic keeps close together: to witness, to preserve, and to have charge over. Allah watches what He has made, keeps a record of it, and holds authority over what He watches.

The Quran also uses the word for the Quran itself, calling it muhaymin over the scriptures before it — a guardian that confirms what was true in them and stands over what was altered.

## Living by this name

Nothing done in private is done unobserved. The name turns worship inward: consistency when no one is looking is the natural response to being always seen.

It is also a comfort rather than only a warning. Being watched by the One who is also merciful means no wrong done to you has gone unrecorded.
