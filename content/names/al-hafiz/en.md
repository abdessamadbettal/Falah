Al-Hafiz is the Preserver — the One who guards what He has made and keeps a record that nothing falls out of.

## What the name means

Hifz is both protection and retention: to keep a thing safe, and to keep it in memory. Allah is Hafiz in both senses — He preserves creation in existence and preserves every deed in the record.

The Quran applies it to His guarding of the revelation itself: We sent down the Reminder, and We are its guardian.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ told Ibn Abbas: guard Allah and He will guard you. The guarding a servant does is obedience; the guarding he receives is protection.

Its response is to entrust what you cannot protect yourself. See also [[al-muhaymin]] and [[al-wakil]].
