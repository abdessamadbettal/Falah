Al-Ghaffar is the One who forgives again and again — whose covering of sin outlasts the servant's returning to it.

## What the name means

The root gh-f-r means to cover and to shield; the mighfar was the helmet a warrior wore. Forgiveness here is not overlooking but covering — the sin is concealed, its consequence lifted, the servant left unexposed.

The form ghaffar is one of repetition. It is not one pardon but a habit of pardoning.

## Living by this name

Nuh ﷺ built his call on it: ask forgiveness of your Lord, He is ever ghaffar — and then promised rain, wealth and children as its fruit. Istighfar is treated as an opening, not only an apology.

Its human echo is covering the faults of others rather than publishing them. See also [[al-ghafur]] and [[at-tawwab]].
