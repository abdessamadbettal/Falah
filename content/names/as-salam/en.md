As-Salam is the One who is Himself free of every flaw and defect, and from whom all safety and peace reach creation.

## What the name means

Salam in Arabic means soundness and wholeness — freedom from harm, defect and decay. The name carries both directions at once: Allah is Himself utterly sound, untouched by weakness or need, and He is the source from which peace reaches anything that has it.

It is the name from which the greeting of Islam is taken. To say as-salamu alaykum is to ask that a name of Allah settle over another person.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ used to say after every prayer: O Allah, You are As-Salam, and from You is peace. The words place the source correctly — the calm a believer is looking for is not manufactured, it is received.

Its practical form is to be someone others are safe from. The Prophet ﷺ defined the Muslim as the one from whose tongue and hand people are secure, which is this name worked out in ordinary conduct.
