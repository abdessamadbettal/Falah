Al-Hakam is the Judge — the One whose verdict is final and whose ruling no court can review.

## What the name means

A hakam in Arabic is the arbitrator both sides accept. Applied to Allah it means more: not the judge chosen from among others, but the only one whose judgement is binding without appeal.

The Quran frames it as a rhetorical question — shall I seek other than Allah as judge, when it is He who sent down the Book to you explained in detail?

## Living by this name

It settles what a Muslim does when a ruling is unwelcome. The Quran makes acceptance of the verdict, not merely agreement with it, the sign of belief.

It also comforts the litigant who cannot get justice on earth: every case is heard eventually. See also [[al-adl]] and [[al-haqq]].
