Al-Jabbar is the One whose will prevails over all — and who mends what is broken, the same word carrying both.

## What the name means

The root j-b-r means to set a fracture. Arabs called the splint that mends a broken bone a jabirah. From the same root comes compulsion — a will that cannot be resisted. Allah is both: irresistible in His decree, and the One who restores what has been shattered.

## Living by this name

When a human being is called jabbar the Quran uses it as blame, because a creature has no right to overpower. The name belongs to Allah alone in its full sense, and the response is submission rather than imitation.

It is the name for the broken — bereaved, wronged, bankrupt of hope. The One who compels is the One who mends. See also [[al-aziz]] and [[al-mutakabbir]].
