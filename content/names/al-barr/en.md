Al-Barr is the Source of All Goodness — kind and generous to His servants, in what they earn and what they do not.

## What the name means

Birr in Arabic is expansive goodness — the same word used for kindness to parents. Applied to Allah it means good that arrives widely, including to those who have not deserved it.

The verse pairs it with mercy after describing the fear the believers lived with: indeed He is al-Barr, ar-Rahim.

## Living by this name

It answers the assumption that Allah's default is severity. The Quran gives the name at the end of a passage about people who were afraid, as the reason their fear ended well.

Its response is birr toward parents and others — the same word, scaled to a servant. See also [[ar-rahim]] and [[al-karim]].
