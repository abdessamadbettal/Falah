Al-Aziz is might that cannot be overcome — strength joined to honour, never bullying and never defeated.

## What the name means

The root ayn-z-z carries three related senses: rarity, strength, and unassailability. Something aziz is precious because it is scarce, powerful because it is strong, and out of reach because nothing can overpower it. Allah is all three in the absolute.

The Quran almost always pairs the name with another — al-Aziz al-Hakim, the Mighty, the Wise; al-Aziz ar-Rahim, the Mighty, the Merciful. The pairing matters: might without wisdom is tyranny, and the Quran refuses to leave the impression.

## Living by this name

Honour is sought from where it actually is. The Quran says that whoever desires izzah should know it belongs entirely to Allah, which quietly ends the search for it in status or wealth.

It also frees a believer from needing to win every exchange. The one whose honour rests with Al-Aziz can afford to let an argument go.
