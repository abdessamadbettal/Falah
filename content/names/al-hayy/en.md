Al-Hayy is the Ever-Living — life that had no beginning, has no end, and depends on nothing.

## What the name means

Every living thing but Allah received its life and will return it. Al-Hayy names life that is not a gift held temporarily but an attribute belonging to the essence.

It is almost always paired with al-Qayyum. The Prophet ﷺ used the two together in distress — Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum, by Your mercy I seek relief.

## Living by this name

The Quran draws the practical conclusion: rely upon the Ever-Living who does not die. Reliance on the living is safe; reliance on the dying is not.

Its response is to keep the heart's dependence where it will not be interrupted. See also [[al-qayyum]] and [[al-baqi]].
