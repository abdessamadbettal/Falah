Dhul-Jalali wal-Ikram is the Possessor of Majesty and Honour — awe and generosity named together, in one title.

## What the name means

Jalal is majesty that humbles; ikram is honour that is both given and deserved. The phrase refuses to separate them: the same One who overwhelms is the One who honours.

It appears twice in Surah ar-Rahman, once mid-surah and once as its closing words — the surah of divine favours ending on the pairing.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ instructed that this name be used persistently in du'a — ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram — pairing reverence with request.

Its response is worship that is neither casual nor despairing. See also [[al-jalil]] and [[al-karim]].
