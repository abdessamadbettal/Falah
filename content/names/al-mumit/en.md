Al-Mumit is the Bringer of Death — the One who decrees death for every soul at its appointed time.

## What the name means

Imatah is to cause death. The Quran frames it as decree rather than accident: no soul dies except by Allah's permission, at a term appointed and written.

The definite name is not in the Quran; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, always paired with al-Muhyi, since giving and taking life are one authority.

## Living by this name

The pairing removes fear's usual grip. If the term is fixed, caution does not extend it and courage does not shorten it.

Its response is preparation rather than dread — the Prophet ﷺ called death the destroyer of pleasures and told us to remember it often. See also [[al-muhyi]] and [[al-warith]].
