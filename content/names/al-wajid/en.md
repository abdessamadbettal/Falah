Al-Wajid is the Perceiver — the One who lacks nothing and finds whatever He wills, whenever He wills.

## What the name means

Wujd in Arabic is both finding and having means. Al-Wajid therefore says that nothing is missing from Allah and nothing is beyond His reach — the opposite of the poverty every creature lives in.

The name is not in the Quran as a divine noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the sense is carried by verses on Allah's self-sufficiency.

## Living by this name

It names what a creature is not. The Quran addresses people as the poor before Allah, and Allah as the one free of need — a permanent asymmetry.

Its response is to ask from the One who has, rather than from those who are asking too. See also [[al-ghaniyy]] and [[as-samad]].
