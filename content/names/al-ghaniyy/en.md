Al-Ghaniyy is the Self-Sufficient — needing nothing from anyone, while everything needs Him.

## What the name means

Ghina is freedom from need. In creation wealth is relative; in Allah it is absolute — He does not benefit from obedience nor is He diminished by disobedience.

The Quran presses the asymmetry: O people, you are the ones in need of Allah, and Allah is al-Ghaniyy, al-Hamid.

## Living by this name

It settles why worship is commanded. Nothing is added to Allah by it; the benefit returns entirely to the one who worships.

Its response is contentment — the Prophet ﷺ said richness is richness of the soul, not abundance of goods. See also [[as-samad]] and [[al-mughni]].
