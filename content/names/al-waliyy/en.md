Al-Waliyy is the Protecting Friend — the ally of the believers, close to them and acting on their behalf.

## What the name means

Wilayah in Arabic is nearness that carries responsibility: the wali is the one whose closeness obliges him to act for you. The name is therefore warmer than protector and stronger than friend.

The Quran states the direction of the relationship: Allah is the Wali of those who believe, bringing them out of darkness into light.

## Living by this name

It reorders alliances. The Quran forbids taking as awliya those who would pull you away, precisely because the word means more than acquaintance.

Its response is closeness through what draws it — the obligatory acts first, then the voluntary. See also [[al-wali]] and [[al-hafiz]].
