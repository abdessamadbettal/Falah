Al-Khabir is the All-Aware — the One who knows the inner reality of things, not only their surface.

## What the name means

Khibrah in Arabic is knowledge gained from the inside — what an expert knows about his craft that an observer does not. Allah's khibrah is not acquired, but the sense of interiority holds: He knows what a thing is beneath what it appears to be.

It pairs naturally with al-Latif, and with hearing and sight, closing the outer and inner registers together.

## Living by this name

It makes reputation a poor substitute for reality. A deed dressed up for onlookers is known for what it is, and a quiet one is not overlooked for being quiet.

It also urges caution in judging others, whose interiors you do not have. See also [[al-alim]] and [[al-latif]].
