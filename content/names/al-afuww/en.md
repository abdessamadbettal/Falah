Al-Afuww is the Pardoner — who erases the sin altogether, leaving no trace to be recalled.

## What the name means

Scholars distinguish afw from maghfirah: forgiveness covers the sin, pardon effaces it. What is covered still exists; what is pardoned does not.

The Quran pairs it with power to remove any suggestion of weakness: Allah is ever Afuww, Qadir — pardoning while fully able to punish.

## Living by this name

Aisha asked the Prophet ﷺ what to say if she found Laylat al-Qadr, and he taught her: O Allah, You are Afuww, You love pardon, so pardon me. The name is placed at the year's most valuable moment.

Its response is to pardon others the way you are asking to be pardoned. See also [[al-ghafur]] and [[at-tawwab]].
