Al-Mu'id is the Restorer — the One who brings creation back after it has passed away.

## What the name means

I'adah is to return a thing to what it was. Paired with al-Mubdi', the two name one cycle: origination and restoration, the second following the first as a matter of course.

The name is not in the Quran as a noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the verb recurs throughout.

## Living by this name

The Quran argues from the easier case: it is He who begins creation and then repeats it, and that is easier for Him. Resurrection is not presented as the harder miracle.

Its response is to take the hereafter as certain rather than probable. See also [[al-ba-ith]] and [[al-mubdi]].
