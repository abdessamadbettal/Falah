At-Tawwab is the Accepter of Repentance — who turns to the servant first, then accepts his turning back.

## What the name means

Tawbah is turning. The remarkable feature of this name is that the Quran uses the same verb for both parties: Allah turns to the servant so that the servant may turn to Him.

The form is intensive — repeated acceptance, however often the return is needed.

## Living by this name

The order matters. Adam ﷺ received words from his Lord before he repented: the enabling came first, which is why repentance is itself a gift rather than a fee.

Its response is to return immediately rather than waiting to feel worthy. See also [[al-ghafur]] and [[al-afuww]].
