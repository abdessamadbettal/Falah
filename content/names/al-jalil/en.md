Al-Jalil is the Majestic — possessing the grandeur before which creation falls silent.

## What the name means

Jalal is majesty: the aspect of Allah that inspires awe rather than intimacy. Classical scholars pair it with jamal, beauty, and say the believer's heart needs both — awe without beauty becomes terror, beauty without awe becomes presumption.

The name is not in the Quran as a standalone noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while jalal appears in Dhul-Jalali wal-Ikram.

## Living by this name

It restores seriousness to worship. Prayer performed casually is not measured against our habits but against the One being addressed.

Its response is reverence — the quality the Quran says is missing when hearts have hardened. See also [[dhul-jalali-wal-ikram]] and [[al-azim]].
