Al-Ahad is the Unique — one in a way that admits no division, no plurality and no comparison.

## What the name means

Arabic distinguishes wahid, one of a kind that could have others, from ahad, one that excludes the very possibility. Ahad cannot be halved, added to, or matched.

The Quran uses it once, as the opening of the surah the Prophet ﷺ said equals a third of the Quran: say, He is Allah, One.

## Living by this name

The surah continues by ruling out what people habitually attach to God: He begets not, nor was He begotten, and there is none comparable to Him. The name is defined by negation because every positive image would mislead.

Its response is worship stripped of intermediaries. See also [[al-wahid]] and [[as-samad]].
