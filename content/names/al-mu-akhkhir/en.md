Al-Mu'akhkhir is the Delayer — the One who defers whom and what He wills, always by wisdom.

## What the name means

Ta'khir is to put back. Its clearest instance is the deferral of reckoning: the Quran says Allah delays them to a Day when eyes will stare in horror — a delay that is not neglect.

Like its pair, the name is not in the Quran as a noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi and appears in the Prophet's ﷺ supplication.

## Living by this name

It interprets waiting. An answer withheld may be an answer deferred to a better moment, which is why the Quran couples delay with a fixed term rather than with abandonment.

Its response is patience that keeps asking. See also [[al-muqaddim]] and [[al-halim]].
