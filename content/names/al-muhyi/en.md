Al-Muhyi is the Giver of Life — the One who brings the living out of the dead, in bodies and in hearts.

## What the name means

Ihya is to give life. The Quran presents it as a repeated, visible sign rather than a distant claim: dead earth revived by rain is offered as the proof of the resurrection to come.

The definite name is not in the Quran; it appears in construct — the One who gives life to the dead — and reaches the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi.

## Living by this name

The Quran also uses life for guidance: respond to Allah and the Messenger when he calls you to what gives you life. Being alive and being guided are treated as one thing.

Its response is to ask for the heart's revival as readily as for the body's health. See also [[al-mumit]] and [[al-hayy]].
