Ar-Raqib is the Ever-Watchful — the One whose observation never lapses and from whom nothing is out of view.

## What the name means

A raqib in Arabic is a sentry posted to watch. The name says Allah's watch is permanent and total — not surveillance that catches, but presence that never leaves.

The Quran opens Surah an-Nisa with it, immediately after commanding awareness of Allah and of family ties: indeed Allah is ever, over you, Raqib.

## Living by this name

Isa ﷺ is quoted in the Quran using it about his own community: I was a witness over them while I was among them, but when You took me, You were the Raqib over them. The watching does not depend on any human being remaining.

Its response is muraqabah — worship as though watched, because you are. See also [[al-basir]] and [[ash-shahid]].
