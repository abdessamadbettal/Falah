Al-Muqtadir is the Omnipotent — power that prevails and carries out what it intends.

## What the name means

The form is more emphatic than al-Qadir: not only able, but able to bring about and to overwhelm. The Quran uses it at moments of decisive action.

It closes the account of Pharaoh's people: We seized them with the seizing of one Mighty, Muqtadir — power described after the event, not as a threat.

## Living by this name

It is also used gently: the righteous are with a Sovereign, Muqtadir. The same power that seizes is the power that hosts.

Its response is to fear consequences without despairing of mercy. See also [[al-qadir]] and [[al-aziz]].
