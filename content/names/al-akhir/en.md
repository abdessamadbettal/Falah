Al-Akhir is the Last — after whom there is nothing, and who remains when everything else has ended.

## What the name means

As al-Awwal denies a beginning, al-Akhir denies an end. The Prophet ﷺ completed the definition in the same supplication: You are the Last, so there is nothing after You.

The four names of that verse — First, Last, Manifest, Hidden — are given together in one line of Surah al-Hadid, describing Allah from every direction at once.

## Living by this name

It puts a limit on grief for what is lost. Everything else is temporary by definition; only one thing is not.

Its response is to invest in what will still be there. See also [[al-awwal]] and [[al-warith]].
