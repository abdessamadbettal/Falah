Al-Baqi is the Everlasting — who remains when everything else has perished.

## What the name means

Baqa is permanence. The name is the counterpart of fana, passing away, and the Quran states the contrast in one line: everyone upon it will perish, and there remains the Face of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honour.

The name is not in the Quran as a divine noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the verb carries it.

## Living by this name

It supplies the Quran's standard for value: what is with Allah is better and more lasting. Lasting is offered as a reason, not as consolation.

Its response is to weigh choices by what survives them. See also [[al-akhir]] and [[al-warith]].
