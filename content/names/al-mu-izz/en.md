Al-Mu'izz is the Honourer — the One who grants might and standing to whom He wills.

## What the name means

From the same root as al-Aziz. Where that name describes Allah's own unassailable might, this one describes His giving of it: honour that arrives as a grant, not as an achievement.

The Quran gives the act in Al Imran — You give sovereignty to whom You will, and You honour whom You will — while the name enters the list through at-Tirmidhi.

## Living by this name

The verse names the giver, which settles where honour is sought. Umar's reported words carry the sense: we were a lowly people whom Allah honoured by Islam, and if we seek honour elsewhere, Allah will humble us.

Its response is to stop trading dignity for approval. See also [[al-aziz]] and [[al-mudhill]].
