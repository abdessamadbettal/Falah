Al-Mu'min is the One who gives security to His creation and who confirms the truth of what He has revealed.

## What the name means

The root a-m-n gives both iman, belief, and aman, safety — and this name carries both. Allah is the One who makes His messengers' truthfulness certain by the signs He sends with them, and the One who grants safety to those who take refuge in Him.

Scholars note the double sense deliberately: the security He gives is what makes belief reasonable, and belief is what opens the door to His security.

## Living by this name

The believer's share is to be trustworthy — to be someone whose word can be relied on and whose presence makes others feel safe. The Prophet ﷺ said the believer is the one people trust with their lives and their wealth.

It is also the name to hold when fear is the dominant feeling: safety is not the absence of danger but the presence of the One who grants it.
