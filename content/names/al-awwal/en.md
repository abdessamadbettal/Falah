Al-Awwal is the First — before whom there is nothing, with no beginning to His being.

## What the name means

First here is not first in a sequence but prior to sequence itself: there was no time before Him in which He was not.

The Prophet ﷺ explained it in the plainest possible terms: You are the First, so there is nothing before You — the definition given as a negation.

## Living by this name

It closes the question of what came before. Any answer that puts something earlier has stopped speaking about Allah.

Its response is to trace every blessing back past its immediate cause. See also [[al-akhir]] and [[al-baqi]].
