Al-Wali is the Governor — the One who owns and manages every affair of creation.

## What the name means

Where [[al-waliyy]] stresses nearness and support, al-Wali stresses charge and administration: the one who runs the affair, not merely the one who is close to you.

The Quran uses it where protection is denied to the wrongdoers: they will have no wali besides Him — no one to take charge of their case.

## Living by this name

It reframes what a person can control. The Quran repeatedly separates the doing, which is ours, from the outcome, which is not.

Its response is diligence without the burden of guaranteeing results. See also [[al-waliyy]] and [[al-wakil]].
