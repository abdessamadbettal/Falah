Al-Maajid is the Illustrious — abundant in glory, honour and generosity together.

## What the name means

Al-Maajid and [[al-majid]] share a root and differ in form; scholars read this one as broader in extent, the majesty spread over creation rather than considered in itself.

Neither this form nor its meaning is disputed, but the name is not in the Quran as a noun — it reaches the list through at-Tirmidhi, where several lists give only one of the pair.

## Living by this name

It is the reason the Quran never treats Allah's greatness as a reason to expect less from Him. Majesty here is what makes the giving large.

Its response is to raise your expectation of Allah rather than lower it. See also [[al-majid]] and [[al-karim]].
