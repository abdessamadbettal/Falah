Al-Qawiyy is the All-Strong — whose strength is complete, never borrowed and never diminished by use.

## What the name means

Quwwah is strength in the plain sense, but applied to Allah it excludes what strength means in creation: no source it was drawn from, no limit it runs into, no fatigue after exertion.

The Quran uses it where a weak party faces a strong one, closing the account: Allah is Qawiyy, Aziz — strong and unassailable.

## Living by this name

It resizes odds. The believers at Badr are described as few and the outcome is attributed to the name, not to the numbers.

It also warns the strong: strength that will be questioned is on loan. See also [[al-matin]] and [[al-aziz]].
