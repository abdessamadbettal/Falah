As-Sabur is the Most Patient — who does not hasten punishment, though nothing prevents Him from it.

## What the name means

Sabr in a servant is endurance under difficulty. In Allah it cannot mean that, since nothing burdens Him — it means deliberate restraint: withholding a deserved consequence for as long as He wills.

The name is not in the Quran; it reaches the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi. It is close to [[al-halim]], and scholars distinguish them by degree, as-Sabur adding length of forbearance.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ said no one is more patient with harm heard than Allah: people attribute a child to Him, and still He grants them health and provision.

Its response is patience that keeps giving to those who have not earned it. See also [[al-halim]] and [[al-ghaffar]].
