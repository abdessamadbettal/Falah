Al-Halim is the Most Forbearing — the One who does not rush to punish, though He has every power to.

## What the name means

Hilm is restraint held by someone fully able to act. It is not weakness or indifference; the Quran attaches it to knowledge and to forgiveness, so the delay is deliberate rather than distracted.

The verse states the reason for the delay: if Allah were to take people to account for what they earned, He would not leave a creature on the earth's surface.

## Living by this name

It explains why the wrongdoer is not struck down immediately — a mercy the wrongdoer may not recognise as one, since the delay is the space in which repentance is possible.

Its human echo is not being provoked into a response you would regret. See also [[as-sabur]] and [[al-ghafur]].
