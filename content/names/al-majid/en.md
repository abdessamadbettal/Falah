Al-Majid is the Glorious — joining greatness of rank to abundance of giving.

## What the name means

Majd in Arabic is nobility that is both high and generous. Arabs would not call someone majid for status alone: the word requires that the standing be matched by open-handedness.

The Quran attaches it to the Throne — Owner of the Throne, the Glorious — and to the Quran itself, described as majid.

## Living by this name

It rules out the idea that greatness and generosity compete. Where people guard status by withholding, this name states the opposite standard.

Its response is honour shown to others rather than extracted from them. See also [[al-maajid]] and [[al-hamid]].
