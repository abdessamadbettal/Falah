Al-Alim is the All-Knowing — the One whose knowledge covers everything at once, without learning it and without forgetting it.

## What the name means

Human knowledge is acquired, partial and lost. Allah's is none of these: it precedes the thing known, includes what will never happen, and does not increase.

The Quran presses the point through scale — no leaf falls but He knows it — and through interiority: He knows what the breasts conceal, which is knowledge of motive, not only of act.

## Living by this name

It makes sincerity the only sensible policy. An audience that sees the intention cannot be impressed by the performance.

It is also relief for the wronged and the misunderstood: the record does not depend on your ability to explain yourself. See also [[al-khabir]] and [[ash-shahid]].
