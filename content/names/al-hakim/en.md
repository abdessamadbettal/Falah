Al-Hakim is the All-Wise — the One who places everything exactly where it belongs, in creation and in command.

## What the name means

Hikmah is putting a thing in its right place. The root also gives hukm, judgement, and the bridle that steers an animal — wisdom as direction, not merely insight.

Its constant pairing with al-Aziz is the Quran's guard against misreading power: might that is wise is never arbitrary.

## Living by this name

It is the answer to why, when the reason is not shown. The angels' question about creating a species that would shed blood is answered not with an explanation but with a statement of knowledge.

Its response is trust in the decree while still asking for what you want. See also [[al-aziz]] and [[al-hakam]].
