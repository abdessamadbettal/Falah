An-Nafi' is the Benefactor — in whose hand all benefit lies, granted to whom He wills.

## What the name means

Naf' is benefit. Paired with [[ad-darr]], the two names close the circuit: nothing helps and nothing harms except by His permission, which is the heart of tawhid in practice.

It is not in the Quran as a noun; it enters the ninety-nine through at-Tirmidhi, while the meaning is repeated in the Sunnah.

## Living by this name

The Prophet ﷺ told Ibn Abbas that if the whole nation gathered to benefit him, they would benefit him only with what Allah had written for him. The teaching is given to a boy, as a foundation.

Its response is to seek what benefits and ask Allah for it. See also [[ad-darr]] and [[al-mujib]].
