Al-Qadir is the All-Capable — able to do whatever He wills, with nothing outside His power.

## What the name means

Qudrah is ability, and qadar is measure — the same root. Allah's power is therefore never raw force: what He is able to do, He does by measure.

The Quran uses the name where people doubt: say, He is the One able to send upon you a punishment — the ability stated before the outcome is described.

## Living by this name

It sets the horizon of du'a. Nothing you might ask for is disproportionate to the One asked, and the Prophet ﷺ said to ask with certainty of being answered.

Its response is to stop editing your requests down. See also [[al-muqtadir]] and [[al-qawiyy]].
