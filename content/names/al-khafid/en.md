Al-Khafid is the Abaser — the One who brings low the arrogant and those who set themselves against the truth.

## What the name means

Khafd is to lower. As a divine act it is not humiliation for its own sake but the correction of a false claim: whoever raises himself above his station is returned to it.

The Quran gives the act rather than the name — the Day of Judgement is described as khafidah rafi'ah, bringing low and raising up — which is why this name reaches the famous list through at-Tirmidhi's narration.

## Living by this name

It is the reason the Quran ties destruction to arrogance rather than to weakness. Pharaoh's downfall is presented as the natural end of a claim, not as bad fortune.

Its response is to lower yourself before you are lowered. See also [[ar-rafi]] and [[al-mutakabbir]].
