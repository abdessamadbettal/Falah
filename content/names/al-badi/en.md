Al-Badi' is the Incomparable Originator — who creates in ways without precedent, from no prior model.

## What the name means

Ibda in Arabic is invention with no antecedent, which is why the word for a religious innovation shares the root. The name says Allah created without copying, because there was nothing to copy.

The Quran uses it in construct: Originator of the heavens and the earth — the two largest things named, to set the scale.

## Living by this name

It answers a limited imagination about what Allah can bring about. The Quran follows the name with the decisive clause: when He decrees a matter, He only says to it Be, and it is.

Its response is to keep asking after the options have run out. See also [[al-khaliq]] and [[al-mubdi]].
