Al-Wadud is the Most Loving — the One who loves His righteous servants and is loved by them.

## What the name means

Wudd in Arabic is love that shows: affection expressed in act, not merely held in the heart. The form is both active and passive — the One who loves and the One who is loved.

The Quran places it beside forgiveness in one verse and beside the Throne in another, joining tenderness to majesty rather than setting them apart.

## Living by this name

It corrects the idea that Allah is only to be feared. Shuayb ﷺ appeals to it while calling his people back: my Lord is Merciful, Loving.

Its response is worship out of love rather than only out of fear, and love for others for His sake. See also [[ar-rahim]] and [[al-barr]].
